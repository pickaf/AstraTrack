HYG Stellar Database v4.1 — David Nash / Astronexus
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database
Source: hyg/CURRENT/hygdata_v41.csv, retrieved 2026-10-12.
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
AstraTrack adaptation: selected stars (magnitude <= 8 over the entire sky), reduced fields and rounded coordinates.
The adapted catalogue is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and available at sky-catalogue.json.
Rows: [HIP identifier (negative HYG id if absent), RA degrees J2000,
declination degrees J2000, V magnitude, proper name].
The gifted star's separate position and V magnitude come from SIMBAD HIP 46684.

Stellarium western sky culture — Stellarium's team
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures/tree/master/western
Retrieved 2026-10-12. Upstream license: Text and data: CC BY-SA.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/
AstraTrack adaptation: 88 constellation line figures, Italian names,
HIP identifiers only; no illustrations or boundary data used in the app.
Adapted data remains CC BY-SA, available at sky-constellations.json.
Original data and license description archived in research/sources/.
Additional HYG v4.1 adaptation: Rigel, Polaris and Vega coordinates and distances,
converted to this scene's galactic frame in galaxy-landmarks.json (CC BY-SA 4.0).
Sagittarius A* reference: ESO https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2208-eht-mw/
Additional named stars in galaxy-landmarks.json use the same source/license.

Gaia DR3 local star field — ESA/Gaia/DPAC
https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/data-release-3
Gaia data are open and free to use with credit to ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission
Gaia, processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC).
Funding for DPAC has been provided by national institutions, in particular
the institutions participating in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement.
References: Gaia Collaboration et al. (2016), A&A 595, A1;
Gaia Collaboration et al. (2023), A&A 674, A1 (Gaia DR3).
Retrieved 2026-10-13 from the ESA Gaia Archive TAP service.
AstraTrack adaptation: 47,503 sources; G absolute magnitude estimate <= 1.5,
parallax >= 1.6 mas, parallax_over_error >= 10; within 1300 light years
of the midpoint between the Sun and the gifted binary.
Distances use inverse parallax; no extinction, zero-point or proper-motion
correction. Catalogue positions at Gaia epoch J2016.0, mapped to scene axes.
Not a complete census or an unbiased measure of stellar density.
Adapted data: galaxy-local-stars.json. Rows: [x, y, z in light years, absolute G magnitude estimate].
Positions rounded to 0.0001 light years (storage precision, not accuracy).
Original query, rows and per-row source IDs are retained in research/sources/.

HYG v4.1 additional adaptation: galaxy-sky-references.json (CC BY-SA 4.0,
David Nash / Astronexus). Muscida, Dubhe, Merak, Talitha, Giausar, Phecda, Megrez, Menkalinan and Almaaz: catalogue RA/Dec J2000
projected at the gifted binary distance for illustrative context only.
The p coordinates are display positions, not physical distances.
